UNIVERSITÉ PARIS-SACLAY · 2023–2025

Field investigations

Measuring, characterising, interpreting.

Maisse, Hallue and Crau: three university field projects on aquifer hydrodynamics and water quality.

01 / CONTEXT

Context

These university projects apply hydrogeological investigation methods: groundwater level observations, hydraulic tests, sampling and interpretation of measurements.

02 / APPROACH & CONTRIBUTION

Approach & contribution

01

Maisse · Aquifer hydrodynamics

Groundwater level measurements and pumping tests in 3 boreholes. Estimation of hydrodynamic parameters in a multilayer aquifer system and interpretation of transfers between aquifer levels.

02

Hallue · Catchment hydrology

Piezometric mapping, sampling, physicochemical and streamflow measurements. Porchet infiltration tests.

03

Crau · Water quality

Study of an aquifer affected by landfill leachate as part of a university field project on groundwater quality.

Project setting

University field investigations in the Hydrology and Hydrogeology Master’s programme · Université Paris-Saclay · 2023–2025.
