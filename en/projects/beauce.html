GEOPS · RESEARCH INTERNSHIP · 2023

Nitrates in the Beauce aquifer

Connecting water quality and hydrogeological context.

Nitrate time series, public databases and mapping to investigate a multilayer aquifer.

01 / CONTEXT

Context

In an agricultural setting, nitrate concentrations can be investigated in relation to aquifer structure. This project examines borehole time series and the potential role of low-permeability formations in groundwater protection.

02 / APPROACH & CONTRIBUTION

Approach & contribution

01

Gathering the data

Using ADES and InfoTerre to acquire and structure hydrogeological records and nitrate time series.

02

Placing observations in space

QGIS mapping to compare water quality observations with the hydrogeological context of the boreholes.

03

Interpreting the time series

Analysis of temporal changes and study of the role of low-permeability formations, connecting spatial data with water quality observations.

Project setting

Research internship at GEOPS, Université Paris-Saclay · May–June 2023. Supervision: Christelle Marlin.

Methods & tools

QGISADESInfoTerreNitrate time seriesMultilayer aquifer

The role of low-permeability formations is presented as a research objective; no quantified protective effect is claimed here.
