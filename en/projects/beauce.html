GEOPS · RESEARCH INTERNSHIP · 2023

Nitrates in the Beauce aquifer

Connecting water quality and hydrogeological context.

Nitrate time series, public databases and mapping to investigate a multilayer aquifer.

01 / CONTEXT

The hydrogeological question

In the Beauce multilayer aquifer, I investigated the protective role of low-permeability formations. I used ADES nitrate time series and InfoTerre geological information, then mapped the boreholes in QGIS. The question was whether an aquifer layer’s position beneath low-permeability cover could help explain the concentrations observed.

Putting the time series in context

Two boreholes close together at the surface may sample different aquifer layers. Their time series therefore need to be considered alongside sampled depth, the presence and continuity of the cover, and genuinely comparable periods. Lower concentrations beneath cover would be consistent with a protective effect. The history of inputs and possible mixing between layers also need consideration.

02 / APPROACH & CONTRIBUTION

Approach & contribution

01

Gathering the data

Using ADES for nitrate time series and InfoTerre for the geological context of the boreholes.

02

Placing boreholes in context

QGIS mapping to connect measurement locations with their setting and the aquifer layer sampled.

03

Building meaningful comparisons

Examining shared time periods and differences in context before discussing concentrations and the role of the cover.
